Power On Your Console

When the console finishes booting, you will be greeted by WiiFlow.

WiiFlow acts as a central hub for all installed games, allowing you to easily browse and launch titles.
You can navigate using the Wii-remote or a GameCube controller and use the 'A' or 'B' button to continue to next page or return to the previous.

This is the screen you should see:

At the bottom of the screen, you will see six icons.
from left to right

  • 1 – Categories
  • 2 – Favorites
  • 3 – Settings
  • 4 – Home
  • 5 – Source
  • 6 – Disc Drive

Below is an explanation of what each icon represents and what it is used for.

Categories & favorites

I paired these together since they both work in similar ways, both are used to quickly find games you're looking for. when you click on a game you will see these options appear on the right side.

If you click the 'STAR' it will add the game to your favorite, which can be view on the previos screen by clicking the favorites icon at the bottom of the screen.

Next to that is the categories button, which allows you to sort your titles into different sections.
(i.e. fighting, racing, shooters, ect.)
From the previous screen if you click the Categories icon it will pull up all the sections you have created and you can search your games that way.

Settings

Now if you received a Modded Wii from Infinite Confusion in 2026 and on, then your setting will be locked by a 4 digit code (Parental Code)
the code for your console will be located on the bottom of the console. We lock them to prevent kids from potentially harming the consoles. for the most part there isnt much you will need to do in the Settings and is best left alone.

However, if you are comfortable with navigating and have a deeper understanding then it should be no problem but we are not responsible for any damage caused by changing the settings.

Home

The 'Home' icon will bring up a second menu

You can also reach this menu by pressing the start button on the controller.

From here you only need to worry about 2 options
'Shutdown' & 'Exit To'

Shutdown will, you guessed it, turn off you console.

Exit to, will allow you to exit to the homebrew channel or the original System Menu.

If you exit Wiiflow and want to get back, turn your console off and back on and you will be brought back to Wiiflow.

Sources

This button's icon changes depending on what source you are viewing.
However you doesnt represent what you're currently viewing and instead shows what the next option is. For your console there are 3 options it cycles through.

Gamecube

Wii

Homebrew Channel

Each section will display the games related to there section
(i.e. Gamecube will bring up gamecube games, same with Wii and Homebrew)

Homebrew will shows Emulators like not64 (Nintendo 64 emulator) & snes9xgx (Super Nintendo emulator)

Disc Drive

The last icon is how you play games from the Disc Drive.
simply incert whatever game disc you wish to play then click the Disc Drive icon and it will launch/play the game

Let's play a Game

To play a game all you need to do is navigate to the game you wish to play, then click 'A' twice to launch the game.

For Wii titles, you will see a loading bar appear at the bottom, once it fully loads it will launch the game.

GameCube games work a little different. If you are playing a GameCube game for the first time then you will be brought to the 'Nintendont' screen which is what we use to play GameCube games. 'Nintendont' will compile the games data, then it will launch the game. After you do this for a game, it will automatically launch the next time, skipping the 'Nintendont' screen.
